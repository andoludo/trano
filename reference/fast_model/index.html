    FastModel

    FastModel is the trano library of fast models close to Buildings: from the same YAML file as Buildings and IDEAS, it generates a model whose physics follow Buildings.ThermalZones.Detailed.MixedAir and the Buildings hydronic and ventilation components, written so that rumoca translates it into a CasADi ODE \(\dot x = f(t, x, u, p)\). The same model simulates in OpenModelica and in CasADi, where trano_optimization estimates its state and solves optimal control problems with IPOPT.

    The library comes in two configurations of the same components and templates:

    • FastModel, the closed loop: the controllers of the YAML (thermostatic valves, pump, three-way valve, boiler and heat pump controls, ideal heating and cooling) are part of the model, written without events, and the water flows follow the pressures of the circuits. Its only inputs are the weather and the internal gains: it simulates the building as the Buildings and IDEAS models do;
    • FastModelMPC, for optimal control: the controllers are left out and the controls of the systems are inputs of the model, which the optimisation decides; the flows are set by the valves.
    trano create-model house.yaml FastModel       # closed loop: house.mo with building_mpc and its runnable building
trano simulate-model house.yaml FastModel     # simulates building with OpenModelica
trano create-model house.yaml FastModelMPC    # the model for optimal control, its controls as inputs

    This page describes the components of the library and their equations, what the library does not model, and how close and how fast it is, ending with the most complex YAML file it simulates compared with Buildings and IDEAS. The FastModel tutorial shows how to use the model in CasADi.

    FastModel before the closed loop

    Until the closed loop was added, FastModel was the model for optimal control. That model is now FastModelMPC: code that builds an optimal control problem with Library.from_configuration("FastModel") takes Library.from_configuration("FastModelMPC").

    How a model is built

    FastModel is used as trano uses Buildings and IDEAS: a Modelica library, trano/library/FastModel.mo, and one template per element of the YAML file, trano/elements/library/models/fast_model/*.yaml. The library is embedded in every generated package; the constructions and glazings of the YAML are written as records of its Data package. The generated package holds two models:

    • building_mpc, the building: an explicit ODE whose inputs are the weather data and the internal gains, and, with FastModelMPC, the controls of the systems. It holds no table, no event and no component of another library: this is the model rumoca translates;
    • building, the runnable model: building_mpc fed with the weather file (the Buildings reader, which also gives building_mpc the location of the site), the occupancy schedules and the external data of the network: with FastModelMPC, the controls replayed from it (each held at 1 without data); with FastModel, the set points of the MonitoredSetpoint emission controls.

    Walls, windows and floors are elements of their own connected to the bus of their zone, as in IDEAS. The templates render each system in the mode of the library (library in the templates): with FastModel, each controller of the YAML is a component of FastModel.Controls connected to the system it drives, and every water component is pressure-driven (pressureDriven=true); with FastModelMPC, each controllable system declares its control as a top-level input of building_mpc named <element>_y (bounded to 0..1) or <element>_Q (a heat flow rate, bounded by the nominal power), and the controllers are not rendered: their decisions are the inputs.

    Rules for an explicit ODE

    Every component keeps a few rules, so that the assembled model translates into \(\dot x = f(t, x, u, p)\):

    • every node joined by connections has a heat capacity, or is solved in closed form inside its component (the radiant star of a room, the mixing of water flows);
    • the equations are smooth (once continuously differentiable): power laws, maxima and table interpolations are regularised, conditions are inside functions or noEvent;
    • water flows are set (FastModelMPC): each valve sets the flow of its branch, a mixing valve the share of supply water, mass balances the rest; the production (boiler, heat pump) or the ventilation unit closes the balance of its circuit, one per circuit. Or they are pressure-driven (FastModel): each component has a pressure node of small compliance at its inlet, so that the pressures are states and every flow an explicit function of them (see water circuits);
    • controllers have no event (FastModel): hysteresis is a fast bistable state, limits are smooth, PI controllers have anti-windup and a smooth reset;
    • a port with several connections is an array, one element per connection, sized by the template: the inlets of a component mix, its outlets leave at its outlet temperature;
    • parameters computed by functions (the conductance of a radiator, the polynomial fits of the window optics, the scaling of the heat pump tables) take values settled at translation.

    Components and equations

    The notation: \(T\) temperatures [K], \(Q\) heat flow rates [W], \(\dot m\) mass flow rates [kg/s], \(A\) areas, \(c_p\) the specific heat capacity of water (4184 J/(kg.K)) or of air (1006 J/(kg.K)), \(\rho\) densities (1000 and 1.2 kg/m3), \(\sigma\) the Stefan-Boltzmann constant.

    Smooth functions

    The library replaces the non-differentiable functions of Buildings by the regularised ones of Buildings.Utilities.Math.Functions:

    • regStep(x, y1, y2, ε): a cubic step from \(y_2\) (\(x < -\varepsilon\)) to \(y_1\) (\(x > \varepsilon\)); smoothMax, smoothMin and smoothLimit are built on it;
    • positivePart(x, δ) \(= \tfrac12\left(x + \sqrt{x^2 + \delta^2}\right)\), a smooth \(\max(x, 0)\);
    • the natural convection law \(Q = A\,c\,|\Delta T|^{4/3}\) is written \(Q = A\,c(\Delta T)\,\Delta T\,(\Delta T^2 + \Delta T_0^2)^{1/6}\), with \(\Delta T_0 = 0.1\) K and the coefficient \(c\) blended smoothly between its values for a surface warmer or colder than the air.

    Weather and solar irradiance

    BoundaryConditions.Weather takes the seven channels of a weather file or a forecast (TDryBul, TBlaSky, winSpe, winDir, HDirNor, HDifHor, HGloHor) and computes the solar position from the model time, as Buildings.BoundaryConditions.SolarGeometry:

    \[ \delta = \arcsin\!\left(-\sin 23.45^\circ \cos\frac{2\pi (t/86400 + 10)}{365.25}\right), \qquad t_{sol} = t - t_{zone} + \frac{\lambda\,43200}{\pi} + E(t), \qquad \omega = \frac{2\pi}{24}\left(\frac{t_{sol}}{3600} - 12\right) \]
    \[ \cos\theta_z = \cos\varphi \cos\delta \cos\omega + \sin\varphi \sin\delta \]

    with \(E\) the equation of time, \(\varphi\) the latitude and \(\lambda\) the longitude, and the Perez coefficients \(F_1\), \(F_2\) of the diffuse sky from the sky clearness and brightness (the eight clearness bins joined by smooth steps). BoundaryConditions.TiltedSurface gives the irradiance on a surface of tilt \(\beta\) and azimuth \(\gamma\):

    \[ H_{dir} = \max(\cos\theta\, H_{dir,nor}, 0), \qquad H_{dif} = H_{dif,hor}\left[(1 - F_1)\frac{1 + \cos\beta}{2} + F_1 \frac{\max(0, \cos\theta)}{\max(\cos 85^\circ, \cos\theta_z)} + F_2 \sin\beta\right] + H_{glo,hor}\,\rho_g\frac{1 - \cos\beta}{2} \]

    where \(\theta\) is the incidence angle on the surface and \(\rho_g = 0.2\) the ground reflectance. The site (latitude, longitude, time zone) comes from the weather file in building; in building_mpc it is a parameter.

    Opaque surfaces

    Envelope.OuterWall, BoundaryWall, AdiabaticWall and InternalWall conduct heat through the layers of their construction, with a node at each layer boundary (\(n\) layers, \(n + 1\) nodes). The capacity of a node is half of each adjacent layer, the conductance of a layer \(k/x\):

    \[ C_j = A\left(\tfrac12 x_{j-1}\rho_{j-1} c_{j-1} + \tfrac12 x_j \rho_j c_j\right), \qquad G_j = \frac{A\,k_j}{x_j} \]
    \[ C_1 \dot T_1 = Q_{sol} - Q_{ext} - G_1 (T_1 - T_2), \qquad C_j \dot T_j = G_{j-1}(T_{j-1} - T_j) - G_j (T_j - T_{j+1}), \qquad C_{n+1} \dot T_{n+1} = G_n (T_n - T_{n+1}) - Q_{int} + Q_{rad} \]
    • Outer surface (OuterWall): absorbed sun \(Q_{sol} = \alpha_{sol} A (H_{dir} + H_{dif})\); exchange with the outside \(Q_{ext}\) as Buildings.HeatTransfer.Convection.Exterior (TemperatureWind) and SkyRadiationExchange: natural convection, forced convection \(h_F = 2.537\, W R \sqrt{P v / A}\) for a square surface (\(P = 4\sqrt A\)), with \(W\) = 1 windward, 0.5 leeward and 0.75 without wind (smooth in the wind direction and speed) and \(R = 1.67\) for a rough surface, and the long-wave exchange with sky and ground linearised around their mean radiant temperature \(T_{env} = \left(F_{sky} T_{sky}^4 + (1 - F_{sky}) T_{out}^4\right)^{1/4}\), \(F_{sky} = (\pi - \beta)/\pi\): \(Q_{lw} = 4\sigma\varepsilon A T_{env}^3 (T_1 - T_{env})\).
    • Floor on ground (BoundaryWall): the outer surface is held at the ground temperature of the YAML (10 degC by default), as trano does for Buildings' datConBou, or at the outdoor air temperature (outdoor_air variant).
    • Party wall (AdiabaticWall): no heat crosses the outer surface.
    • Wall or floor between zones (InternalWall): each face exchanges with its own zone; side b faces the other way (tilt \(\pi - \beta\), a floor seen from below is a ceiling).
    • Inner surface: natural convection to the room air \(Q_{int}\) with the coefficients of Buildings (1.3 for a wall; 0.76 or 1.51 for a ceiling or a floor depending on whether it is warmer than the air), or a fixed coefficient; long-wave and solar exchange with the room \(Q_{rad}\) (see the zone).

    Windows

    Envelope.Window models each pane of its glazing as a node, the gas gaps as conductances, and the frame as a conductance between the room air and the outside, after Buildings.HeatTransfer.Windows:

    \[ C_i \dot T_i = Q_{abs,i} + G_{i-1}(T_{i-1} - T_i) - G_i (T_i - T_{i+1}) \quad (\text{outer pane: } - Q_{ext},\ \text{inner pane: } - Q_{int} + Q_{rad}) \]
    • \(G_i = A_{gla} / (R_{pane,i}/2 + R_{gap,i} + R_{pane,i+1}/2)\), the glass area \(A_{gla} = (1 - f_{fra})A\);
    • the optics of the glazing are computed by trano from its layers with the algorithm of Buildings (Buildings.HeatTransfer.Windows.Functions, after Finlayson et al.: angular properties of each pane from its normal-incidence values, panes combined by the net radiation method): direct transmittance \(\tau\) and absorptance \(a_i\) of each pane at the incidence angles 0, 10, ..., 90 degrees, and their hemispherical values; the gap resistances follow EN 673. The library fits a quartic in \(\cos\theta\) through the ten angles (least squares, at translation), so that the optics are smooth in the sun position: \(Q_{abs,i} = A_{gla}\left(H_{dir}\, a_i(\cos\theta) + H_{dif}\, a_i^{hem}\right)\);
    • transmitted radiation, distributed in the room by the zone: \(Q_{tra,dir} = A_{gla} H_{dir} \tau(\cos\theta)\), \(Q_{tra,dif} = A_{gla} H_{dif} \tau^{hem}\);
    • outside: \(Q_{ext} = A_{gla}(4 + 4v)(T_1 - T_{out}) + Q_{lw}\) (the wind-dependent film coefficient of the Buildings window model and the sky exchange above);
    • frame: \(Q_{fra} = \dfrac{f_{fra} A}{1/h_e + 1/U_{fra} + 1/h_i}\,(T_{air} - T_{out})\), taken from the room air.

    Zones

    Zones.Zone balances the air of the room, exchanges long-wave radiation between its surfaces through a radiant star, and distributes the transmitted solar radiation, after Buildings.ThermalZones.Detailed.MixedAir:

    \[ m_{sen}\,\rho c_p V\,\dot T_{air} = \sum_i Q_{con,i} + Q_{con,emitters} + A_{flo}\, q_{con} + \rho c_p V \frac{n_{ach}}{3600}(T_{out} - T_{air}) + \sum_k \dot m_k c_p (T_{sup,k} - T_{air}) \]

    with \(Q_{con,i}\) the convective heat of each surface, the internal gains \(q_{con}\) per floor area, the infiltration \(n_{ach}\) (air changes per hour, infiltration variant) and the supply air of a ventilation unit (VentilatedZone; the extract air leaves at \(T_{air}\)). The long-wave exchange is linearised at \(T_0 = 20\) degC: each surface \(i\) has the conductance \(G_i = 4\sigma T_0^3 \varepsilon_i A_i\) to the radiant star, whose temperature balances the radiant gains:

    \[ T_{rad} = \frac{\sum_i G_i T_i + Q_{rad,emitters} + A_{flo}\, q_{rad}}{\sum_i G_i}, \qquad Q_{rad,i} = G_i (T_{rad} - T_i) + k_{dir,i} Q_{tra,dir} + k_{dif,i} Q_{tra,dif} \]

    The transmitted diffuse radiation is absorbed in proportion to \(\alpha_i A_i\) (\(k_{dif,i} = \alpha_i A_i / \sum_j (\alpha_j + \tau_j) A_j\)); the direct radiation hits the floors first, and what they reflect is spread over the other surfaces, as SolarRadiationExchange of Buildings. A radiant emitter sees the mean surface temperature \(\sum_i G_i T_i / \sum_i G_i\).

    Water circuits

    The water ports carry a mass flow rate and the temperature of the water crossing them. A component mixes its inlets (regularised by \(\dot m_{small} = 10^{-6}\) kg/s so that a closed branch keeps a defined temperature) and its outlets leave at its outlet temperature:

    \[ T_{in} = \frac{\sum_k \dot m_k T_k + \dot m_{small}\, \overline{T_k}}{\sum_k \dot m_k + \dot m_{small}} \]

    Set flows (FastModelMPC):

    While the pressures settle, a flow may reverse for a moment: the components mix the positive parts of their inflows and carry the positive part of their flow, so that the temperatures stay finite. The circuit has no fixed pressure: the compliances hold the mass of the water, and only the pressure differences matter.

    Radiator (RadiatorEN442_2 of Buildings): \(n_{ele} = 5\) water and metal elements in series, each emitting by the EN 442 power law, a share \(f_{rad}\) radiant:

    \[ \frac{C}{n_{ele}}\dot T_i = \dot m c_p (T_{i-1} - T_i) - Q_{con,i} - Q_{rad,i}, \qquad Q_{con,i} = (1 - f_{rad})\, UA_{ele}\, \Delta T_i (\Delta T_i^2 + \Delta T_0^2)^{(n-1)/2} \]

    with \(\Delta T_i = T_i - T_{air}\) (radiant part: \(T_i - T_{rad}\)), \(n = 1.24\), \(C = \rho V_{wat} c_p + 500\, m_{dry}\) and the water volume and dry mass of Buildings (5.8e-6 m3/W and 0.0263 kg/W of nominal power). As Buildings at initialisation, \(UA_{ele}\) is solved (by bisection, at translation) so that the radiator emits its nominal power at its nominal water and room temperatures.

    Boiler (BoilerPolynomial), with set flows the reference of its circuit: the part load ratio \(y\) is the control (FastModelMPC) or comes from Controls.BoilerTemperatureControl (FastModel),

    \[ Q_{fuel} = y\,\frac{Q_{nom}}{\eta_{nom}}, \qquad C\,\dot T = \dot m c_p (T_{in} - T) + \eta(y, T)\, Q_{fuel} + UA\,(T_{amb} - T), \qquad \dot E_{fuel} = Q_{fuel} \]

    with the efficiency curves of Buildings (constant, polynomial in \(y\), or quadratic-linear in \(y\) and \(T\)), its water volume, dry mass and heat loss to the boiler room.

    Air-to-water heat pump (AirToWaterTableData2D with the Vitocal 251-A08 data of Buildings, scaled to the capacity of the YAML) charging a mixed buffer, with set flows the reference of its circuit: the compressor speed \(y\) is the control (FastModelMPC) or comes from Controls.HeatPumpControl (FastModel),

    \[ T_{con,out} = T + \Delta T_{con}\, y, \qquad Q_{con} = y\, s\, \tilde Q(T_{con,out}, T_{out}), \qquad P_{el} = y\, s\, \tilde P(T_{con,out}, T_{out}) \]
    \[ C\,\dot T = \dot m c_p (T_{in} - T) + Q_{con} + UA\,(T_{amb} - T), \qquad \dot E_{el} = P_{el} \]

    with \(s = Q_{nom} / \tilde Q(T_{set}, T_{out,des})\) the scaling of the tables, \(\tilde Q\) and \(\tilde P\) the EN 14511 tables made differentiable (quadratic in the condenser temperature through the rows at 35, 45 and 55 degC, linear in the source temperature with the kinks smoothed over 1 K), \(C\) the buffer and the condenser water, and \(UA\) the insulation of the buffer. Its coefficient of performance at the current temperatures, \(\mathrm{COP} = \tilde Q / \tilde P\), is a variable of the model, defined also when the compressor is off.

    Ventilation

    Ventilation.Duct carries \(\dot m = y\,\dot m_{nom}\), the speed \(y\) of the fans of its unit travelling along the ducts and through the zones. Ventilation.HeatRecoveryUnit (trano's system D: constant-effectiveness heat exchanger and two constant-pressure fans), the reference of its air loop, with the fan speed \(y\) as its control:

    \[ T_{sup} = T_{out} + \varepsilon\,(T_{ext} - T_{out}) + \frac{P_{sup} + \varepsilon P_{ret}}{\dot m_{nom} c_p}, \qquad P_{fan} = y\,(P_{sup} + P_{ret}), \qquad P = \frac{\Delta p_{nom}\,\dot m_{nom}}{\rho\,\eta_{fan}} \]

    where \(T_{ext}\) is the mixed extract air of the zones and the fans heat the air (the supply fan after the exchanger, the return fan before it).

    Controls (closed loop)

    FastModel.Controls holds the controllers of trano's Buildings models, written without events so that the closed-loop model stays an explicit, smooth ODE:

    • a hysteresis (CDL Hysteresis) is a bistable state switching within \(\tau = 10\) s, \(\tau \dot y = \mathrm{regStep}\big(u - (u_{high} + (u_{low} - u_{high})\,y)\big) - y\);
    • a PI controller (Modelica LimPID) limits its output smoothly, with back-calculation anti-windup: \(y = \mathrm{lim}(k(e + I))\), \(\dot I = e/T_i + (y - y_{unl})/(k N_i T_i)\); while disabled, its integral relaxes to the reset value in 30 s;
    • the day schedules of set points ({on, off, ...} in seconds of the day) change with smooth steps of 60 s, the time of day taken without events.

    HeatPumpControl supply set point on the heating curve, from TSet at the design outdoor temperature to 30 degC at the heating limit (16 degC); heating season by a hysteresis of ±1 K at the limit; the buffer charges below the set point minus half the band dTHys until it is half the band above; the compressor PI (\(k = 0.1\), \(T_i = 300\) s, minimum speed 0.3) on the condenser outlet, towards the set point plus dTHys

    Electricity and ideal systems

    • Electrical.Photovoltaic (PVSimpleOriented): \(P = A\, f_{act}\, \eta\, \eta_{DC/AC}\, (H_{dir} + H_{dif})\) on the plane of the modules, metered as EPro.
    • HVAC.IdealHeatingCooling (FastModelMPC): the heat flow rate \(Q\) is an input <element>_Q, split between the room air and the radiant star by its radiant share.
    • HVAC.IdealHeater (FastModel): an ideal emitter delivers \(Q = y\,Q_{nom}\), \(y\) from its emission control.
    • HVAC.IdealHeatingCoolingPI (FastModel): a PI controller per mode (\(k = 0.1\) per kelvin, \(T_i = 300\) s) towards the day tables of the heating and cooling set points, limited by the capacities, metered as EHea and ECoo.

    The meters are states in kWh: EFue (fuel), EEle (heat pump electricity), EFan (fans), EPro (PV production); the comfort temperatures are the TAir states of the zones. trano_optimization.Roles.infer reads these roles from the model: an input with a min and a max is a control, any other input a disturbance.

    Limitations

    Not modelled: generating a model stops with a message naming the element. Boilers with a storage tank, water-to-water heat pumps, chillers, domestic hot water tanks, batteries, EV chargers, VAV boxes and their controls, air handling units other than the balanced unit with heat recovery (system_d), and power sensors.

    Simplifications:

    • Controllers without events (FastModel). The controllers follow those of trano's Buildings models, but a hysteresis switches within 10 s instead of at an instant, and the safety controls of the heat pump (minimum on and off times) are not modelled: the heat pump starts and stops more often than in Buildings, which shows in the water temperatures (about 2 K RMSE) and in the hour-by-hour heat of the radiators (6–16 % CV(RMSE)), less in the zones and the meters. The boiler controls default and complex charge a storage tank, which FastModel does not model: a boiler without storage driven by them always has demand, its temperature control alone sets its firing.
    • The controls are inputs (FastModelMPC). The controllers are left out: the optimal control decides, or the runnable model replays the controls of the external data. Without data, building holds every control at 1.
    • Flows. With set flows (FastModelMPC), each valve sets the flow of its branch: the sharing of flow between branches (pump curves, valve authorities, balancing) is not computed. With pressure-driven flows (FastModel), the resistances are linearised at their nominal flows (as Buildings does with linearized=true), and the components that have none in Buildings (sensors, the condenser of the heat pump) have a small one. One production per hydraulic circuit, and mixing three-way valves only: diverting valves are not modelled.
    • Ventilation units are balanced (supply = extract) and run at constant pressure rise; infiltration is a constant air change rate (no wind- or stack-driven air flows).
    • The envelope follows Buildings, not IDEAS: the floor on ground sees a fixed ground temperature (IDEAS models the ground with ISO 13370), and overhangs and side fins of the windows are not modelled. The long-wave exchange in a room is linearised at 20 degC, the interior convection uses the natural convection laws of Buildings.
    • No moisture: sensible heat only, with constant air properties; the co2 occupancy variant only brings its heat gains.
    • The heat pump is the Vitocal 251-A08 of Buildings scaled to the capacity of the YAML, heating only, without the safety controls (minimum on and off times) of Buildings.
    • Tools: the runnable model needs OpenModelica (or Dymola) and Buildings for the weather reader; the CasADi export of the connected building_mpc, closed loop or not, needs rumoca 0.10.3 (rumoca main up to c74cddf, 2026-10-13: the later commits stop the export of these models at residual_equations). The closed-loop model exports too, but its fast controllers and pressure nodes make it stiff: CVODES takes about 100 s per simulated day for the renovated house, where OpenModelica runs its four weeks in under two minutes.

    Accuracy

    The validation simulates the Buildings (or IDEAS) model of a YAML file with its own controls and compares it with FastModel in two ways: the closed-loop FastModel with its own controllers, and the FastModelMPC model replaying the controls of Buildings (the heat of the ideal emitters, or the flow of each radiator branch, the position of each three-way valve, the boiler part load ratio or the heat pump speed and the fan speed; everything else is simulated by FastModel).     Speed

    Four weeks of January, OpenModelica 1.26.9 (DASSL, tolerance 1e-6), on the same 4-core machine:

    Model Translation and compilation [s] Simulation [s]
    Buildings 182 886
    IDEAS 167 373
    FastModel (building, replaying the controls) 24 99
    FastModel closed loop (building, its own controllers)* 10 105
    FastModelMPC in CasADi (building_mpc, CVODES, inputs held over 5-minute steps) 30 (rumoca) 9

    * Measured later on another 4-core machine, where the replay simulates in 84 s: the closed loop costs about a quarter more than the replay.

    FastModel simulates the four weeks 9 times faster than Buildings and 4 times faster than IDEAS in OpenModelica, and about 100 times faster than Buildings once translated to CasADi. The Buildings and IDEAS runs record the signals the replay needs every minute, the FastModel run every 15 minutes.

    The CasADi model is the same model as the OpenModelica one: simulated with the inputs of the OpenModelica run, its zone temperatures stay within 0.014 K of it over the four weeks.

    Optimal control. A day-ahead problem of the house (hourly steps, the 9 controls, comfort at 20 degC from 7 to 22 h and 17 degC otherwise, electricity bought at 0.30 and sold at 0.05 per kWh) is solved by IPOPT from the middle of January:

    Radau collocation points per hour (CollocationSettings.degree) NLP variables IPOPT iterations Solve time [s]
    1 about 9 600 100 39
    2 about 14 200 97 117
    3 (default) about 18 700 165 380–475

    The model itself costs little (about 2 s of evaluations in the slowest solve): the time goes into the factorisations of the KKT system, whose Hessian blocks couple the 189 states at every collocation point. For a model of this size, fewer collocation points per step, or a shorter horizon, keep the solve within a minute.
