# FastModel against Buildings

The models of the `FastModel` library are made for optimal control: they translate into a CasADi ODE. How close they
stay to the Buildings model of the same YAML file is measured by `validation/mpc`:

```bash
python -m validation.mpc compare 600FF residential_before --models FastModel
python -m validation.mpc report
```

The same command compares the flat RC models of the `mpc` library (`--models R1C1 R3C2 R4C3 ISO13790`).

For each case the Buildings model is simulated, then the runnable model of the other library **replays** what the Buildings
systems did:

* the heat of the ideal emitters of each zone, for the envelope cases;
* the flow of each radiator branch, the position of each three-way valve and the part load ratio of the boiler
  (or the speed of the heat pump compressor), for the hydronic cases, and the air flow of the ventilation unit.
  Everything else is simulated by the `FastModel` model: the production, the water circuits, the radiators, the
  ventilation, the PV array and the zones.

Weather, solar irradiance (direct, Perez diffuse) and occupancy come from the same Buildings blocks in both
models. The zone air temperatures are compared every 15 minutes; the radiator heat hour by hour (ASHRAE
Guideline 14 CV(RMSE) and NMBE); the water temperatures of the circuits every 15 minutes. The thermostatic
valves of the hydronic cases modulate the flows faster than that, so their Buildings reference is recorded every
minute and the controls are replayed as means over 5 minutes, which conserves the heat the water carries.

## Thresholds

| Stage | Compared outputs | Threshold |
|---|---|---|
| Envelope, single zone (BESTEST) | free-floating zone temperature | RMSE ≤ 1.0 K |
| Envelope, several zones | heated zone temperatures | RMSE ≤ 0.5 K |
| Emitters | radiator heat | CV(RMSE) ≤ 15 %, NMBE within ±5 % |
| Distribution and production | water temperatures, zone temperatures | RMSE ≤ 1 K, zone RMSE ≤ 0.5 K |

## Reading the results

**Envelope.** The `FastModel` zone follows Buildings within 0.33–0.39 K (RMSE) in the free-floating BESTEST
cases, against 2.3–7.3 K for the RC models of the `mpc` library on the same YAML file: the RC models aggregate the envelope into one
to three capacities whose parameters come from the constructions, without calibration, and they lose the
fast exchanges between the window, the floor and the air that drive a free-floating zone. In the heated and
cooled BESTEST cases the replay of the Buildings heat is open loop, so that any difference accumulates until the
envelope removes it; the `FastModel` zone stays within 0.41–0.65 K.

**Multi-zone envelope.** With walls and floors shared between zones, the three-zone house stays within
0.18–0.49 K of Buildings (RC models: 1.2–2.8 K).

**Hydronics.** In the three hydronic houses (the residential house before renovation with its gas boiler, after
renovation with its heat pump, ventilation and PV, and the heat pump test house), only the branch flows, the three-way valve positions and the boiler part
load ratio or the heat pump compressor speed are replayed; the production, the water circuits, the radiators and
the zones are simulated by the `FastModel` model. The radiators deliver the heat of Buildings within 0.6–4.4 % CV(RMSE)
and a bias below 1 % (the 21 % of the nearly unheated bedroom 3 of the heat pump house is on 4 kWh in four
weeks); the zones follow within 0.11–0.33 K; the boiler supply and the heat pump buffer within 0.3–0.5 K. The meters of
the renovated house follow Buildings over the four weeks: heat pump electricity −1.8 % (−1.6 % in the heat pump
test house), fans −0.6 %, PV
production 0.0 %.

**Ventilation.** The supply air of the renovated house's unit stays within 0.30 K of Buildings, 0.29 K too warm on
average: in Buildings the extract flow is 3 % below the supply flow (part of the supply leaves through the
infiltration paths of the zones, as the pressures set it), so that the heat exchanger recovers on a smaller
stream; the `FastModel` unit is balanced. The
return temperatures (1.2–1.5 K, without bias) mix the outlets of radiators whose flows the thermostatic valves
modulate within the replay interval.

## Differences that remain

* Each `Valve` sets the flow of its branch, `y·m_flow_nominal`, as the replay needs and as an MPC decides it; in
  Buildings the flow that a valve opening gives depends on the pressures of the whole circuit, which the
  `FastModel` model does not compute.
* The heat pump interpolates the EN 14511 tables in the source temperature as Buildings does, smoothed at the
  knots, and is quadratic in the condenser temperature (within 1 % of the tables up to 55 °C); its condenser
  outlet is approximated explicitly from the buffer temperature.
* Ventilation units are balanced (see above).
